Signature Project

Chengdu Metro — "Mamen River Dragon" Mural

Our handcrafted mosaic mural at Chengdu Metro Institute of Technology Station reproduces the Late Jurassic "Mamen River Dragon" scene with the unique craft of manual mosaic inlay. The station has become a popular photo spot for young visitors.

From detail iteration during production to full-quality evaluation and size-split installation — our team manages the entire artistic scheme for large construction sites.
